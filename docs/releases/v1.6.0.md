## AltLoad 1.6.0

Logs, a choice of developer team, more control over entitlements and JIT, and three new languages.

### New
- **Logs.** Tools › Support › Logs shows what AltLoad did: installs, refreshes, sign-ins, pairing, JIT, AltServer and LocalDevVPN. Filter by category, show errors only, search, copy, share as a file or clear it. Apple ID emails are masked, and passwords, codes, tokens and pairing files are never logged.
- **Copy Log.** When an install fails, tap Copy Log to copy the error, your AltLoad and iOS versions and the last lines of the log, ready to paste into an issue.
- **Developer team.** If your Apple ID belongs to more than one team, pick which one signs your apps in Tools › Certificates. Automatic keeps using the first team. The choice is used for installs, refreshes and certificate actions.
- **Entitlements per app.** Each installed app can use the shared set or its own. There are presets for emulators and JIT apps and for VPN and network tools, plus search. Capabilities that need a paid team are marked, and are skipped on a free team. A new card shows what the last signed profile actually granted.
- **JIT.** Pin apps and see recent ones at the top of the JIT screen. A new Shortcuts and Siri action, Open App with JIT, lets you pick the app from a list. Long-press AltLoad on the Home Screen to start JIT for your most recent or pinned apps.
- **Languages.** AltLoad is now in Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Japanese, as well as English, German, French, Italian, Russian and Chinese.
- **Welcome screen.** A short welcome on first launch, with the official download site.

### Fixed
- **"Tunnel connect timed out" when installing.** AltLoad now retries the connection through LocalDevVPN up to three times. If it still fails, the error says what to try: turn LocalDevVPN off and on, turn off other VPN or proxy apps, and check Developer Mode.

### altpair
- **Team choice.** `--team <ID>`, or pick from a list when your Apple ID has more than one team. altpair remembers the pick.
- **More reliable.** Downloads retry and resume, network steps retry, and altpair checks that LocalDevVPN is connected before starting.
- **Clearer.** Numbered steps, a longer `--help`, `--verbose`, a summary at the end, and errors that say what to do next.

Official downloads are only on GitHub and mirazbakis.github.io/AltLoad.
