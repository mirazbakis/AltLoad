AltLoad is a free iOS app that does on the device what a computer would otherwise do. It pairs your iPhone or iPad with itself, reaches it through LocalDevVPN, signs in to Apple's developer services with your Apple ID, and signs and installs AltStore, AltStore Beta, SideStore or SideStore inside LiveContainer, including their nightly builds.
